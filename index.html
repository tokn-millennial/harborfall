How to play

Reach 100 Workers. Houses need a market in range and fish. Upgrade a full house to hold Workers, who also need bread. After the win, Workers can become Artisans, who also need cloth.

Buildings that make things need staff, and staff come from your residents. If the town is short of staff, everything is made more slowly: build more houses, or select a building you can spare and press "Close for now".

Left clickPlace, select, upgrade or demolish
Left dragMove the view. With Road picked: lay a line of road. Hold Shift to flip its corner.
Right or middle dragMove the view
Right click, EscPut the tool away
Mouse wheel, + and -Zoom
W A S D, arrowsMove the view
Home or CBack to the start
SpacePause or resume
1, 2, 3Speed 1x, 2x, 4x
Letter on a buttonPick that tool
Z, Ctrl+ZUndo your last action

A red ! on a building means it has no road or no market. An amber ! means it is waiting for something. A green ▲ means a house is ready to upgrade. A grey ‖ means you have closed the building.
